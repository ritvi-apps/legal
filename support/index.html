Common questions

Charades

I paid for a pack but it is locked.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Packs are tied to the store account that bought them, so make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account. If it is still locked after restoring, email us with your receipt.
I changed phone. Do I pay again?
No. Purchases are permanent and tied to your store account. Install the app and tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
Tilt to Play is not responding.
Turn it on in Settings, then hold the phone flat against your forehead. Tip it down toward your chest to score and away to skip. If nothing happens, your device may not report motion while the screen is locked — keep the app in the foreground.
Is there a subscription?
No. There are no subscriptions, no ads and no daily limits. Every category includes a free Starter pack, and paid packs are one-time purchases.

Tic Tac Toe

The Hard AI never loses.
That is correct behaviour. Hard uses a minimax search and plays perfectly, so a draw is the best result available. Try Medium for a winnable game.
How do I remove the ads?
The app is free and supported by ads, and there is currently no paid option to remove them. Every mode and difficulty is unlocked from first launch.

All apps

How do I request a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, not by us, so refunds have to go through them. On iOS use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android use Google Play → Account → Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history. Email us if you get stuck and we will help where we can.
What data do you collect?
It varies per app — see the privacy policy linked above for each one. Charades and Tic Tac Toe keep your scores and settings on your device and do not upload them.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Email firodiya.ritesh@gmail.com. We read everything.
